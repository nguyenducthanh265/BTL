# Final dataset QA findings

Checked live `results/raw/` on 2026-11-06, beginning **13:03:10 UTC**; all eight fingerprints were checked again and unchanged at **13:06:10 UTC**. This is a technical QA note, not a release or scientific-significance claim. No dataset was edited. Match aggregation uses the project's shared `experiments.report.load_games` validator and canonical identities.

## Integrity and reproducibility

1. **53,000 structurally valid unique match records; zero rejected records:** Tic-Tac-Toe 43,000, Gomoku 6,700, Xiangqi 3,300. Xiangqi includes 3,200 planned and 100 supplementary games. All eight input files had identical byte counts and SHA256 before and after the analysis read. The final experiment audit owns full rule replay and frozen-snapshot delivery.
2. **All 53,000 recorded seeds match `runner.match_seed`.** No cross-experiment copies were found when comparing the same agents and index together with full move sequence, times and node totals. This narrow duplicate check does not establish independence of trajectories or historical producer-code provenance.
3. **Four fresh seeded game regenerations match current code exactly.** Calling `runner.run_one(task)` in memory reproduced `seed`, `a_first`, `a_score`, `plies`, `a_nodes`, `b_nodes`, and the complete `moves` string for the four configurations below. Timings were deliberately excluded. These are samples, not certification that every historical row was generated by the current checkout.

4. **Node experiment: 173 records = 162 completed, 10 skipped by node limit, one timeout.** Finished algorithms agree on both value and the full set of best moves in all **58 comparable position/radius/depth cases**; one further case has only one finished algorithm. At the empty 3x3 board, Minimax/Alpha-Beta/ordered Alpha-Beta visit **549,946 / 31,621 / 22,294 nodes**. That is 17.39x and 24.67x fewer nodes than Minimax. Ordering's wall time there is 0.0846 s versus 0.0554 s without ordering; node reduction does not guarantee speedup. Gomoku depth-3 radius-2 averages over five common positions are 154,220.6 / 29,427.0 / 3,981.4 nodes; Xiangqi depth-3 over four common positions is 66,712.25 / 16,621.75 / 2,122.25. Skipped or timed-out searches must stay explicit.

## Quantitative cross-checks

Score is `(wins + 0.5 * draws) / games` from agent A's perspective. Counts below are **W/D/L**, not percentages. MCTS uses c=1.4 and most-visited final selection. Gomoku uses random rollout; Xiangqi uses cutoff rollout. Gomoku AB1/AB2 also restrict candidates to radius 1/2; Xiangqi AB has no radius restriction.

| Game | Iterations | Versus random | Versus AB1 | Versus AB2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gomoku | 1,000 | 100/0/0 | 0/0/100 | 0/0/100 |
| Gomoku | 5,000 | 100/0/0 | 12/0/88 | 0/1/99 |
| Xiangqi | 1,000 | 47/3/0 | 27/1/22 | 0/0/50 |

5. **Budget improvement depends strongly on the opponent.** Gomoku c=1.4 score against fixed MCTS500 rises from 0.10 at 100 iterations to 0.67 at 1,000 and 1.00 at 5,000 (100 games/cell), while the separate AB2 ladder loses all 100 games at every measured budget, including 5,000. Its separate 5,000-iteration WDL cell has one draw. Xiangqi c=1.4 scores 0.16/0.70/0.96 against MCTS500 at 100/1,000/5,000 iterations (50 games/cell), but only 0.11 against AB2 at 5,000. Equal iteration count is not equal work across games or policies.
6. **Exploration c rankings are descriptive and budget dependent.** At 500 iterations against the fixed MCTS500 reference, c=0.5 scores 0.62 in Gomoku and 0.75 in Xiangqi, versus 0.59/0.46 for c=1.4. At 5,000 iterations Gomoku c=1.4 scores 1.00 and c=0.5 scores 0.99; Xiangqi c=0.5/1.0 scores 1.00 and c=1.4 scores 0.96. No universal optimal c follows from these cells.
7. **Rollout gains require measured-time qualification.** Gomoku heuristic/cutoff at equal 1,000 iterations score 0.70/0.99 against random-rollout MCTS1000, but cost 891.8/442.9 versus 198.8/202.5 ms per own move within those pairings. Reduced-budget heuristic220 scores 0.51 at 174.0 versus 180.0 ms; cutoff500 scores 0.93 at 239.8 versus 222.7 ms. Thus the latter is approximately, not exactly, time matched (+7.7%). Xiangqi cutoff500 scores 0.84 versus random-rollout MCTS500 while costing 283.6 versus 1,406.1 ms per own move in that pairing. These historical wall times were measured under parallel load, not a controlled single-process timing benchmark.
8. **Final-selection and UCT comparisons do not establish one winner.** In direct Gomoku matches, max-W/N versus most-visited scores 0.50/0.61/0.58 at 100/500/2,000 iterations (100 games each). Xiangqi at 500 iterations is 25/5/20, score 0.55. Gomoku UCB1-Tuned c=0.5 scores 0.81 and progressive bias c=0.5 scores 0.75 against standard UCB1 c=1.4 at 1,000 iterations; Xiangqi equivalents at 500 iterations score 0.92 and 0.82. Those low-c comparisons change formula and c together. Holding c=1.4, UCB1-Tuned scores 0.68 in Gomoku and 0.60 in Xiangqi. Rankings across many explored cells are exploratory, not multiplicity-adjusted paired significance results.

## Limits and stale artifacts

- The 3x3 WDL baseline at 1,000 iterations is 191/9/0 against random, 0/193/7 against perfect, and 0/196/4 against full-depth Alpha-Beta. Its c=1.4 `curve_perfect` cell has eight losses, because it is a separately seeded experiment. These are distinct cells and must not be conflated.
- All four c values have zero losses in their 5,000-iteration 3x3 `curve_perfect` cells (200 games each); this finite observation is not a proof of never losing. Stored quality enumeration reports zero blunders at 2,000/5,000 over three seeds and 4,520 reachable nonterminal positions per seed; historical producer provenance and finite-seed limitations still apply.
- Xiangqi has **231/3,300** games reaching at least 200 plies; **224** of those are draws. The seven decisive games can terminate on the cap move. CSV does not record draw reasons, so do not label all 224 as repetition draws or independently distinguish adjudication causes.
- `results/integrity.json` and existing README/audit text were stale during this check: the former still counted only 4,590 Gomoku games. Do not present those older counts as the final snapshot. The final experiment worker must replace or supersede them with its independently validated final capture.
- Wilson intervals applied to fractional draw scores have no guaranteed nominal coverage. First/second roles alternate, many cells are compared, and selection of the best observed setting is optimistic. Keep comparisons descriptive unless a suitable paired or hierarchical analysis is added.
